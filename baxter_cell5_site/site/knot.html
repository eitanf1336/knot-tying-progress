Grab by grab

One square per grab, in order: filled green landed the shape asked, filled red laid another shape, outlined red failed in motion. Amber: the grab was not as asked, yet the rope got there: either it laid the target knot when an in-between shape was asked, or the rope shifted into the shape asked before the next reading. A grab is judged the moment its own motion ends; the reading comes after the photo tour, about 16 to 30 robot seconds later.
